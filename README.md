NEXUS SO

One AI. Infinite Possibilities.

NEXUS AI is a next-generation AI-powered business operating system designed to help individuals and organizations manage multiple businesses, automate workflows, and simplify digital operations through one intelligent platform.

Vision

To build a globally accessible, secure, intelligent, and scalable AI platform that brings business management and automation into one unified experience.

Core Modules

- AI Command Center
- Multi-Business Management
- Social Media Management
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Finance and Analytics
- Online Madrasa Management
- Workflow Automation
- AI Knowledge Management
- Notifications and Reports

Technology Direction

- Android: Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
- UI: Material 3
- Architecture: Clean Architecture and MVVM
- Backend: Cloudflare Workers
- Database: Cloudflare D1
- Version Control: GitHub
- Build Automation: GitHub Actions

Development Principles

- Security first
- Privacy by design
- Free-first infrastructure
- Modular architecture
- Global accessibility
- Reliable and tested functionality
- Google Play policy compliance

Project Status

Project initialization and architecture planning.

NEXUS AI is under active development.
